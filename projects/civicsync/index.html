CIVIC TECHNOLOGY · WORKING PROTOTYPE

CivicSync

AI-assisted civic issue reporting, routing, and accountability, designed around municipal workflows.

From a report
to a trackable record.

CivicSync structures free-text complaints and supports a traceable issue lifecycle.

The citizen interface supports English, Hindi, and Bengali. Citizens can track status and resolution, while authorities work within jurisdiction-scoped queues.

AI assists with complaint understanding. Official status changes, assignment, resolution, and reopening remain governed application actions.

Citizen reporting

Free-text complaints, optional location, and a public tracking identifier.

Authority workflows

Issue transitions, department assignment, resolution evidence, and review of reopening requests.

Engineering

FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite with Alembic, Google Gemini, and a framework-free web interface.

CURRENT SCOPE

A prototype, with clear limits.

The project documentation identifies CivicSync as a working prototype, not a production deployment. This presentation makes no claim of municipal adoption, users, or measured operational outcomes.
